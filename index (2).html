Privacy Policy

Clear information about how SmartTool handles your data.

Effective date: October 16, 2026

1. Introduction

SmartTool is an everyday utility application offering a Smart Calculator (including percentage, discount, and VAT calculations), VAT History, Unit Converter, and Age Calculator. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use the app.

2. Information Collection

SmartTool does not require an account. The app is designed to function without collecting personally identifiable information through its core utility features.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

VAT calculation history may be saved locally on your device so you can view previous calculations. The local history feature does not transmit this information to a SmartTool server. Clearing app data or uninstalling SmartTool may remove saved history.

4. Device Permissions

The current calculator, unit conversion, and age calculation features do not require access to your camera or precise location. Any permissions requested by the installed app should be reviewed against the final release build.

5. Third-Party Services

The core features are not designed to share personal information with third parties. If advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party services are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe their data practices where applicable.

6. Children's Privacy

SmartTool is a general-purpose utility app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through its core features.

7. Security

Locally stored information is subject to the security protections and settings of your device. No method of data storage is completely risk-free.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy when SmartTool's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
